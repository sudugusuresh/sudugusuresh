# 💫 About Me:
With nearly two decades of experience in enterprise software engineering, telecom platforms, CPaaS solutions and cloud-based applications, I have progressed from Java/J2EE development into solution architecture, technical leadership and enterprise delivery.<br/><br>My experience spans designing scalable application architectures, developing microservices and integration solutions, working across Google Cloud Platform and AWS environments, and supporting complex telecom and enterprise implementations. I have also worked on AI-enabled applications, agentic workflows, WhatsApp support agents and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions.<br/><br>Throughout my career, I have worked closely with international clients, business stakeholders and engineering teams, contributing to requirements discovery, architecture discussions, technical demonstrations, RFPs, UAT and deployment coordination. My approach combines technical decision-making with delivery governance, team mentoring, quality assurance and stakeholder collaboration.
